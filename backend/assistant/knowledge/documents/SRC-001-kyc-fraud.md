# SRC-001 — Fake KYC and bank-impersonation fraud
Banks in India never ask customers to update KYC through SMS or WhatsApp links.
A genuine KYC update happens at a branch or inside the bank's official app or website
that you open yourself. Any message claiming your account will be blocked within
24 hours unless you tap a link is a pressure tactic. Do not tap the link, do not
enter passwords or OTPs, and call the bank on its official number instead. If money
has moved, call the cyber helpline 1930 immediately and file a complaint at
https://cybercrime.gov.in.
