# SRC-010 — Understanding Scam Shield verdicts
Scam Shield returns Safe, Suspicious or Scam with a risk score from 0 to 100.
Scores below 40 mean no major red flags were found. Scores from 40 to 70 mean
pause and verify the sender through official channels. Scores of 70 and above
mean the message matches known fraud patterns — do not tap links, do not pay and
do not share codes. The verdict explains itself with highlighted trigger words and
risk signals such as urgency language, shortened links and OTP requests.
