# SRC-002 — UPI, OTP and PIN safety
To RECEIVE money on UPI you never need to enter a PIN. A PIN is only used when
YOU pay someone. Any person asking for your UPI PIN, card OTP, CVV or password
to send you a refund, prize or cashback is committing fraud. Never share OTPs,
even with callers claiming to be bank staff — real bank staff never ask for them.
Check collect requests only inside your own UPI app, and reject requests you did
not expect. Report fraud at https://cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930.
