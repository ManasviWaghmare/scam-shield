# SRC-003 — Spotting phishing links
Check a link before tapping it. Warning signs: shortened links that hide the real
site, strange endings such as .xyz or .top on messages pretending to be banks,
very long links, and links with @ symbols. A safe habit is to open the official
app or type the official website address yourself instead of tapping message links.
Banks and government portals never distribute login or KYC links over SMS forwards.
